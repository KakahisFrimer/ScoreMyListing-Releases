Privacy Policy for ScoreMyListing

ScoreMyListing is a browser extension that scores marketplace listings on Etsy, eBay, and Shopify and optionally rewrites or translates listing text. This policy explains exactly what data the extension handles. The short version: the developer operates no servers, collects no user data, and runs no analytics or tracking of any kind.
What the extension does with data.

Scoring happens entirely on your device. When you open a supported listing page and use ScoreMyListing, the extension reads the public listing's title, tags, description, attributes, and photo count, and computes a quality score and checklist locally in your browser. Listing content and scores are never stored, never transmitted to the developer, and are discarded when you leave the page.
Settings are stored locally on your device. The extension stores your preferences in your browser's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local): your selected AI model, your PRO license code, your own Google Gemini API key, and a small capped error log used only for troubleshooting. This data stays on your device. It is stored unencrypted by the browser's standard storage mechanism, so you should not share your unlocked browser profile with people you do not trust.

AI features communicate only with Google, only at your request. The optional PRO features (Rewrite and Translate) send the listing text you choose to rewrite or translate directly from your browser to Google's Gemini API, using your own API key. This happens only when you press the Rewrite or Translate button. The developer never receives, sees, or stores this text or your API key. Data sent to Google is handled under Google's own API terms and privacy policy, which apply to your use of your Gemini API key. Under Google's free Gemini API tier terms, inputs and outputs may be processed and reviewed by Google to improve their models; if you require different terms, consult Google's API documentation before using AI features.

Payments are handled by Gumroad. PRO licenses are sold through Gumroad. The developer receives your order information from Gumroad (such as your purchase email address) solely to deliver your license code to you manually and to keep a delivery record. The developer does not receive or store your payment card details; Gumroad's privacy policy governs payment data.

Support emails. If you contact the developer by email, your email address and message are used only to respond to you.

What the extension never does
It never collects or transmits your personal data to the developer.
It never uses analytics, advertising trackers, or cookies.
It never connects to, logs into, or modifies your marketplace store accounts.
It never sells or shares any data with third parties.
It never asks for your API key, license code, or passwords inside a webpage; all credential forms exist only inside the browser's Side Panel.
